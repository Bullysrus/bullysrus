French Bulldog Stud Services

Bullys R Us | California-Based Breeding Program

Meet the exceptional French Bulldog sires behind Bullys R Us. Each stud is carefully selected for structure, temperament, genetics, and the qualities we strive to preserve in the breed.

Stud Services and Shipping

We offer stud services for approved females, with fresh chilled semen available for overnight shipment.
Three artificial inseminations are included in the stud fee for local breedings. We are located in Modesto, California.
Fresh chilled semen shipping is available for an additional fee starting at $200, with shipping costs depending on the destination.
International shipping is also available for an additional fee.
Progesterone testing is required before a shipment is sent. Each shipment includes two vials of semen prepared for your female.
If your female does not become pregnant after the first breeding, we offer a complimentary in-person rebreed. Shipping fees still apply to shipped rebreeds.
