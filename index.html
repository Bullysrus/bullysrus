Welcome To Bullys R Us

✦

At Bullys R Us, we are dedicated to raising exceptional French Bulldogs and American Bullies with a focus on health, structure, temperament, and responsible breeding. What began in 2021 with our first American Bully quickly grew into a passion for preserving and improving the breeds we love.

Every puppy is raised with love, daily care, and early socialization to help them become confident, well-adjusted companions. We believe that responsible breeding starts with prioritizing health, quality, and the lifelong happiness of every dog we produce.

Located in California's Central Valley, we are passionate about matching each puppy with the right family while providing guidance and support throughout their journey. Whether you are searching for your next family companion or a quality addition to your breeding program, our goal is to make the experience personal, transparent, and memorable.

We offer nationwide delivery options and stud services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reserve a puppy?

Our puppies are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis. A small deposit is required to secure your chosen companion.

We accept secure payment options including but not limited to Zelle, Cashapp, and ApplePay. We welcome families to meet their puppy in person or FaceTime before making their reservation. Once a deposit has been placed, we are able to hold your puppy for a maxiumum of two weeks while final arrangements are made.

We are excited to help you find the perfect addition to your family and create a smooth, enjoyable experience from reservation to pickup.

What are your available delivery options?

We are happy to offer convenient delivery options for our Bullys R Us families.

Complimentary delivery is available within a 100-mile radius of Modesto, California.

For families located out of state, we offer hand delivery throughout the United States for an additional fee starting at $400. Every puppy is transported with care and safety as our top priority.

All puppy meetings and handoffs will take place in a secure public location to ensure a safe and comfortable experience for both our families and our puppies.

Are puppies fully vaccinated and dewormed?

At Bullys R Us, the health and well-being of our puppies is our top priority.

All puppies will go home with their age-appropriate vaccinations, providing protection against Parvovirus, Distemper, Adenovirus, and Parainfluenza. We deworm all pups 4 times, beginning at 2 weeks and ending at 8 weeks.

Each puppy will also come home with a detailed health record documenting their vaccinations, deworming treatments, and care history. This allows every family to have a clear understanding of their puppy’s health journey from the very beginning.
