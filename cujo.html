About Cujo

Cujo is an amazing example of a Fluffy French Bulldog, known for his teddy bear appearance, compact structure, and unique L4 fluffy coat. His beautiful color, strong build, and loving personality make him a standout part of our program.

Cujo is a proven sire who has produced multiple litters with 6-10 puppies, passing on his desirable traits and exceptional temperament.
