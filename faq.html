Yes. Our Fluffy French Bulldogs are all purebred and fully registered with AKC. The fluffy coat is caused by a naturally occurring genetic trait which influences coat length.
You may have heard terms such as L1 or L4 when discussing fluffy French Bulldogs. These refer to different alleles (genetic variations) associated with coat type. For a French Bulldog to visibly express the fluffy coat, they must inherit two copies of the fluffy gene. This means two short-haired French Bulldogs can carry the gene and produce a fluffy puppy if both parents pass it down.

Yes. Our big rope French Bulldogs are selected with health and function as a priority. Like all French Bulldogs, they are a brachycephalic breed, meaning they naturally have shorter noses and compact airways. Because of this, we believe responsible breeding should focus on structure, airway health, and overall quality of life. We carefully consider health and function when choosing breeding prospects and avoid pairings that may increase the likelihood of excessively oversized ropes, such as breeding two big ropes together.

Fluffy French Bulldogs do not automatically have breathing problems. Like all French Bulldogs, their breathing ability comes down to their individual structure, airway health, and responsible breeding decisions — not simply their coat type. A fluffy coat does not determine whether a French Bulldog can breathe comfortably. Because all French Bulldogs can be sensitive to heat, we encourage families to practice proper heat safety, including avoiding excessive heat, providing shade and water, and monitoring activity during warmer temperatures.
