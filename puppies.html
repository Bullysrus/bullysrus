Why Choose Bullys R Us?

Choosing a puppy is a big decision, and we believe the process should feel just as personal as bringing your puppy home. At Bullys R Us, we take the time to get to know each family, understand what they are looking for, and help make sure the puppy they choose is a good fit for their home and lifestyle.

Our involvement doesn't stop once a puppy is reserved. From their earliest days, our puppies receive daily attention, handling, and positive experiences with people and their surroundings. Before going home, each puppy receives age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming and leaves with health records documenting their care.

We also believe families should have the opportunity to feel confident in their decision. When puppies are available, families can meet them in person or through FaceTime before making a reservation. From the first conversation to pickup or delivery, we stay involved and provide guidance to help make the transition into your home as smooth as possible.
