Our Puppy Process

From the first conversation to bringing your puppy home, we are committed to providing a thoughtful and supportive experience.

01 Finding Your Companion

The journey to finding the right puppy for your family

Application

Start by completing our puppy application or contacting us directly at any of our available messaging options.

Getting To Know Your Family

Every family is unique. We take time to understand your expectations and help ensure our puppies are matched with homes that are the right fit.

Meeting Available Puppies

Families are welcome to meet available puppies in person or through FaceTime before making their reservation.

02 Our Puppy Care

The care and preparation that happens before your puppy comes home

Daily Care

Our puppies receive daily attention, handling, and care to help them grow into confident companions.

Socialization

We provide early experiences and positive interactions to help puppies become comfortable with new people and environments.

Health Records

Each puppy goes home with their health record documenting their care, important details, age-appropriate vaccinations, and complete deworming schedule.

03 Bringing Home Your Puppy

The beginning of your puppy's forever journey

Reservation

Puppies may be reserved once they reach 4 weeks old. A deposit secures your chosen puppy and allows us to begin preparing for their transition home.

Pickup & Delivery

Families may pick up in Modesto, California, or choose from available delivery options. We strive to make every puppy's journey home safe and comfortable.

Transition Tips

Bringing home a puppy is an exciting adjustment. We provide guidance to help families start their relationship with their new companion on the right paw.
