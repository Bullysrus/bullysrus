Our Puppy Process

At Bullys R Us, we are dedicated to raising healthy, happy, and well-socialized French Bulldogs. Our goal is to provide each family with a wonderful companion and a smooth experience from reservation to bringing your puppy home.

Our puppies are available to reserve starting at 4 weeks old and are ready to go home with their forever families at 8-12 weeks.

01

Apply For A Puppy

Begin by filling out our puppy application or contacting us directly through one of our available messaging options.

02

Meet Your Puppy

Families are welcome to FaceTime with available puppies or meet them in person before making their reservation.

03

Reserve Your Puppy

Puppies can be reserved starting at 4 weeks old and are placed on a first-come, first-served basis. A deposit is required to secure your chosen puppy.

04

Puppy Care

All puppies go home with age-appropriate care, including their first DHPP vaccination, deworming, and a health record documenting their care.

05

Bringing Your Puppy Home

Families may pick up in Modesto or choose from our delivery options. We offer complimentary delivery within 100 miles of Modesto and hand delivery throughout the United States.
