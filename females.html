Stella

Fluffy Blue & Tan Merle French Bulldog

About Stella

Stella is a beautiful example of our program, featuring a wonderful temperament and playful personality. Her loving nature is something we strive to preserve in all of our dogs. Her stunning merle coat and luxurious fluffy fur make her a truly special part of Bullys R Us.

Genetics: Fluffy French Bulldog

Color: Blue & Tan Merle

DNA: Carries Coco, Intensity, and Pie

Pedigree: AKC Registered

Diva

Fluffy Black & Tan Merle French Bulldog

About Diva

Diva is our first Dam produced directly at Bullys R Us. We chose her as a very healthy and first pick puppy from her litter. She is an amazing example of a Fluffy French Bulldog. Her fluffy merle curls are soft and luxurious, but her personality and temperament are what truly make her special. She has been calm, loving, and caring since day one.

Genetics: Fluffy French Bulldog

Color: Black & Tan Merle

DNA Testing: At/At D/d B/b CO/co Em/e M/m ky/ky L1/L4 NN

Pedigree: AKC Registered

Temperament: Sweet, loving, calm, playful, and a cuddlebug.

Suri

Lilac & Tan French Bulldog

About Suri

Suri is a beautiful short-haired Lilac & Tan French Bulldog. She has excellent breathing and is very athletic and healthy. Her genetics carry Newshade Isabella and Intensity, creating beautiful features and a unique look. Her shiny lilac coat and soft fur make her stand out among other French Bulldogs.

Genetics: French Bulldog

Color: Lilac & Tan

DNA: Carries Isabella and Intensity

Pedigree: AKC Registered

Carmela

Lilac Tri Merle American Bully

About Carmela

Carmela is an American Bully produced directly by Bullys R Us. She represents our dedication to producing beautiful dogs with exceptional temperaments and loving personalities. Her confident nature, affectionate personality, and loyal temperament make her a wonderful representation of the American Bully breed and an important part of our program.

Breed: American Bully

Color: Lilac Tri Merle

Temperament: Confident, affectionate, and loyal
