Behind The Scenes

From The First Moments To Forever Homes

A look into the dedication, preparation, and care that goes into raising every Bullys R Us puppy.

Before The Puppies Arrive

Raising a litter begins long before puppies are born. Careful planning, monitoring, and preparation are essential to giving both the mother and puppies the best possible start. During pregnancy, our females are closely monitored as they approach delivery. We prepare their whelping space, monitor changes, and pay close attention to their health and comfort. French Bulldogs often require additional care during delivery, and many litters are delivered by scheduled C-section to help ensure the safety of both the mother and her puppies.

Welcoming New Life

The first days of a puppy's life require constant attention and dedication. Newborn puppies are monitored closely to ensure they are nursing properly, maintaining healthy weight, and staying warm and comfortable. Our puppies begin their journey in a clean, safe, and carefully prepared environment where they can grow alongside their mother while receiving individual attention.

Growing Through Early Experiences

As puppies grow, their environment expands with them. Our puppies begin experiencing new sights, sounds, textures, and gentle handling to help build confidence and prepare them for life with their future families. We believe early experiences play an important role in developing happy, well-adjusted companions.

Preparing For Their Forever Homes

Before leaving for their new families, puppies receive age-appropriate care including vaccinations, deworming, and a health record documenting their development and care. Each puppy leaves with the foundation they need for a smooth transition, including feeding information and continued support from Bullys R Us.
