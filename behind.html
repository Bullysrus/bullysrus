Behind The Scenes

From The First Moments To Forever Homes

A look into the dedication, preparation, and care that goes into raising every Bullys R Us puppy.

Before The Puppies Arrive

Raising a litter begins long before puppies are born. Careful planning, monitoring, and preparation are essential to giving both the mother and puppies the best possible start. During pregnancy, our females are closely monitored as they approach delivery. We prepare their whelping space, monitor changes, and pay close attention to their health and comfort. French Bulldogs require their litters to be delivered via scheduled C-section to help ensure the safety of both the mother and her puppies.

Welcoming New Life

The first days of a puppy's life require constant attention and dedication. Newborn puppies are monitored hourly to ensure they are nursing properly, maintaining healthy weight, and staying warm and comfortable. We weigh each pup daily for the first two weeks of life to ensure they are gaining weight properly.

Frenchie pups don't always naturally take to their mothers nipple. They require syringe feeding every 2 hours for the first few weeks of life. That means many nights with little to no sleep, sleeping on the floor with the pups.

Growing Through Early Experiences

As puppies grow, their environment expands with them. Our puppies begin experiencing new sights, sounds, textures, and gentle handling to help build confidence and prepare them for life with their future families. We believe early experiences play an important role in developing happy, well-adjusted companions. Our pups are raised in-home, and adjust to everyday life naturally. We practice safe-socialization with our pups. That means no outside exposure to other dogs before they are fully vaccinated. We use puppy strollers and take them on walks, introducing the outside world from a safe distance.

Preparing For Their Forever Homes

Before leaving for their new families, all puppies receive age-appropriate preventative care and health steps. We deworm our pups once every 2 weeks starting at 14 days old. Each puppy also receives their age-appropriate vaccinations that protect against Parvo and other sicknesses. We keep a health record for each individual pup documenting their development and care, that you will receive with your puppy upon pickup. Each puppy leaves with the foundation they need for a smooth transition, including feeding information, and continued support from Bullys R Us.
