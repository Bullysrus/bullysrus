Behind The Scenes

From The First Moments To Forever Homes

A look into the dedication, preparation, and care that goes into raising every Bullys R Us puppy.

The Breeding Process

A successful litter begins months before puppies are ever born. One of the most important parts of breeding is timing. Unlike humans, dogs are fertile once every 6 months, and can only become pregnant during a very short window in their heat cycle, and breeding too early or too late can result in a missed litter.

To determine the best time to breed, we use progesterone testing. This simple blood test measures the female's progesterone levels, allowing us to accurately track ovulation and identify the optimal breeding window. Rather than relying on dates alone, progesterone testing helps remove the guesswork and greatly improves the chances of a successful pregnancy.

Once the female reaches the appropriate progesterone level, the breeding can take place. Depending on the pairing, this may involve natural breeding or artificial insemination using fresh or shipped semen.

After breeding, the waiting begins. Pregnancy can usually be confirmed by ultrasound around 28 to 35 days after ovulation, allowing us to monitor the developing litter and prepare for the exciting weeks ahead.

Before The Puppies Arrive

Raising a litter begins long before puppies are born. Careful planning, preparation, and around-the-clock monitoring are essential to giving both the mother and her puppies the best possible start in life.

Our females are closely monitored as they approach delivery. We prepare a safe, comfortable whelping space, watch for changes, and pay close attention to their health and well-being every step of the way.

Because French Bulldogs commonly require delivery by scheduled C-section, the final weeks of pregnancy are especially important. We work closely with our veterinarian, monitoring the pregnancy through ultrasounds and reverse progesterone testing to help determine the safest time for delivery. This careful preparation helps give both mom and her puppies the best possible outcome.

Welcoming New Life

The first days of a puppy's life require constant attention and dedication. Newborn puppies are monitored hourly to ensure they are nursing properly, maintaining healthy weight, and staying warm and comfortable. We weigh each pup daily for the first two weeks of life to ensure they are gaining weight properly.
Frenchie pups don't always naturally take to their mothers nipple. They require syringe feeding every 2 hours for the first few weeks of life. That means many nights with little to no sleep, sleeping on the floor with the pups.

Growing Through Early Experiences

As puppies grow, their environment expands with them. Our puppies begin experiencing new sights, sounds, textures, and gentle handling to help build confidence and prepare them for life with their future families. We believe early experiences play an important role in developing happy, well-adjusted companions. Our pups are raised in-home, and adjust to everyday life naturally.

Preparing For Their Forever Homes

Before leaving for their new families, all puppies receive age-appropriate preventative care and health steps. We deworm our pups once every 2 weeks starting at 14 days old. Each puppy also receives their age-appropriate vaccinations that protect against Parvo and other sicknesses. We keep a health record for each individual pup documenting their development and care, that you will receive with your puppy upon pickup. We practice safe-socialization with our pups. That means no outside exposure to other dogs before they are fully vaccinated. We use puppy strollers and take them on walks, introducing the outside world from a safe distance.
